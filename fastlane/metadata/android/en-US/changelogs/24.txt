- New: optional "auto-start after reboot" switch on the main screen. It is off by default — the proxy has no authentication by default, so turning this on without asking would silently open an open SOCKS5 on your LAN every time the device boots. Two conditions must both hold before it starts: the switch is on, and the proxy was still running when the device shut down. Pressing Stop clears that second flag, so a stop is never silently undone
- New: `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` permission (only used for the switch above). Android will show it as a new permission when you update
- Fixed: a boot-time start that fails because the network is not ready yet (common in the first seconds after boot) now retries every 15 seconds up to 5 times instead of giving up and reporting a failure
